Champions of the Arena

In the pit every hero but your party is fair game. Falling here costs no gold.

Waystone travel to another land

Each land's dungeon is a copy of your own: your party shares it, nobody else comes in.

Controls
  • Left click on the ground: walk there (hold to keep walking) · WASD also move
  • Left click a monster: target it and attack · Z targets the nearest (again: the next) · Esc lets go
  • R attack your target. Combos: press again just as a blow lands (the attack slot flashes) for a faster, stronger chain of up to four; too early breaks it. A skill in that moment is stronger too.
  • 1–8 skills: at your target, at the cursor (areas), or on a friend you clicked (heals, blessings)
  • T auto-hunt: your hero fights the monsters around where you switched it on, picks up loot and drinks when hurt. Any move stops it. It runs while the game is on screen: in another tab your hero just waits, and monsters leave it alone.
  • Q healing potion · X mana potion · E talk to people in camp, use waystones and doors
  • I character & bag · K skills: a point every level, in three trees. Spread out or go all in.
  • Right click another hero: invite to your party, or trade with them
  • Every camp has a weaponsmith, an armorer, a provisioner (potions, elixirs, camp scrolls), a banker (one bank for all your heroes) and a notice board. Brom's anvil in Emberwood upgrades items to +7 with recipes.
  • World bosses roam the lands now and then. They leave you alone unless you strike first, they are very hard, and they carry unique items.
  • Enter chat · Mouse wheel zoom · Esc settings · M mute
  • Left thumb anywhere on the left side: move.
  • Tap a monster to target it, a hero to see what you can do with them.
  • Tap the sword to attack your target: tap again just as each blow lands for a combo. Hold it to keep swinging.
  • Skill buttons go at your target (or the nearest foe), heals at the friend you tapped.
  • AUTO hunts on its own around where you are, while the game stays open on screen. Potions by the sword.
  • Skills: a point every level, in three trees. Bag: tap an item to see it.
  • Every camp has merchants, a banker (one bank for all your heroes) and a notice board; Brom's anvil in Emberwood upgrades items.
