Champions of the Arena

In the pit every hero but your party is fair game. Falling here costs no gold.

Waystone travel to another land

Each land's dungeon is a copy of your own: your party shares it, nobody else comes in.

Controls
  • WASD Move
  • Left click Attack toward cursor (hold to keep swinging)
  • 1–4 Skills, aimed at the cursor
  • Q Drink ale (heal)
  • I / B Character & bag
  • E Trade with Wren the merchant, open your stash, or read the notice board (in camp)
  • Accept quests at the notice board, then come back to claim the reward. The golden ! means something is waiting.
  • The crypt door in the graveyard leads down to a dungeon and its boss, Morvain the Lich. Step out of his violet circles!
  • The waystone in camp takes you to other lands (from level 8): Frostfang, Cinderfall and Shadowmere, each with its own boss and dungeon. Dungeons are a copy of your own (your party's).
  • The Arena (by waystone, from level 5): in its pit, heroes fight heroes, all but their own party. No gold is lost there; the champions' board counts the wins.
  • Enter Chat with everyone in the world (Enter again to send, Esc to cancel)
  • Mouse wheel Zoom
  • Esc Settings: music, sound, graphics, leave the game · M mute
  • Walk over loot to pick it up. The camp is safe and heals you fast.
